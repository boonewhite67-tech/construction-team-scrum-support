Team Scrum is the app ICM Construction crews use to run a job site. A team is one job site. The same person can be on several.

Board

Backlog, Sprint Backlog, Doing Now, and Done. Cards carry a title, notes, assignee, due date, priority, and Fibonacci points.

Standup

Yesterday, today, and what is blocking you. Answers post to the crew on that job site. A blocker is highlighted.

Field report

Weekly points, velocity, committed versus completed, and the crew leaderboard.

Sign in with email and a password. There are no ads, no tracking, and no sale of data. The privacy policy says what is stored and how to ask for it to be deleted.
