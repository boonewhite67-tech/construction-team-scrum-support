Privacy policy

What Team Scrum stores, why, and how to ask for it to be deleted.

Home Support Privacy

Last updated 21 November 2026.

Team Scrum is the job-site board, standup, and field report for ICM Construction crews. ICM Construction operates the app. This page covers the Team Scrum app and this website. This website does not set cookies and does not collect analytics.

What the app collects

The app collects information only from people who create an account or join a job site:

  • Email address. Used to sign in with a password through Supabase Auth, to send a password reset, and to match an email invite. The app shows the email on the account. It does not let you edit it.
  • Password. Used only to sign in. Supabase Auth stores it. The app does not put the password in your profile, and this policy’s author cannot read it.
  • Name. The display name teammates see on cards, the standup, and the leaderboard. You can change it in Settings.
  • Account id. The id Supabase assigns to the account. It ties your profile, memberships, cards, comments, standups, and push tokens together.
  • Team and job-site data. Job-site name, timezone, your position (project manager or superintendent), invite codes, sprint dates, capacity, and points committed or completed.
  • Cards and points. Titles, notes, assignee, due date, priority, Fibonacci points, column, and comments.
  • Standup text. What you did yesterday, what you will do today, and any blocker, plus the date and the job site.
  • Push token. An Expo push token for this device, saved so the server can send a teammate Done alert or a standup reminder. It is not an advertising identifier. The app does not read the phone’s advertising id.
  • Notification preference. Whether you have muted other people’s Done alerts on a job site, and whether that job site notifies the crew when a card is Done.

What the app does not collect

No ads. No tracking across other companies’ apps or websites. No sale of data. No analytics product. No precise or coarse location. The job-site timezone is a setting someone chooses, not a reading from the phone. No contacts, photos, video, or audio. Voice transcription is not part of the app. No browsing history, search history, purchases, financial information, health, or fitness data.

Why

The data runs the board, the standup, the field report, invites, and the notifications you allow. Done alerts go to teammates when that job site has alerts on, and they are skipped for anyone who muted them. Standup reminders go to people on that job site who have a push token, at that site’s time and weekdays. People on a job site can see that site’s cards, comments, standup answers, points, and display names. Someone who is not on the team cannot read them.

Who holds it

ICM Construction operates Team Scrum. The account, the job-site rows, and the push tokens are stored in ICM Construction’s Team Scrum Supabase project. Expo delivers the push notification to the device. Supabase and Expo process that data to provide the app. They are not given it for their own advertising. It is not sold.

How long it stays

From Settings, someone who manages the job site can delete that site. That removes the site’s cards, sprints, standups, and queued notifications. Leaving a team removes you from that site. Signing out deletes this device’s push token when the phone can reach the server. The rest of the account stays until you ask for it to be deleted.

Deleting your account

There is no in-app button that deletes the whole account. Email boone-white67-jj3gjy@mail.grokbot.com from the address on the account and ask for the account to be deleted. That request covers the profile, the email, the display name, the account id, the push tokens, and the cards, comments, and standup answers tied to you. Content other people still need on a shared card may remain with the card, or it is removed when someone deletes that job site.

You can also delete a single card or comment you are allowed to remove, leave a job site, or delete a job site you manage. Those actions are in the app and do not by themselves delete the account.

Children

Team Scrum is a tool for construction crews. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change and this page will be updated.
