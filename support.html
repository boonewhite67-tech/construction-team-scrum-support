What the app is

A team in Team Scrum is one job site. Project managers and superintendents share a one-week sprint. The board is one column at a time: Backlog, Sprint Backlog, Doing Now, and Done. Cards have a title, notes, an assignee, a due date, a priority, Fibonacci points, and comments.

The standup asks what you did yesterday, what you will do today, and what is blocking you. Answers go to the people on that job site. Moving a card to Done plays the job site’s sound and a short burst. The Dashboard is the field report: weekly points, velocity, and a crew leaderboard.

FAQ

How do I sign in?

With the email and password you chose in the app. Team Scrum does not use a one-time code or a magic link for sign-in. If you forget the password, use Forgot password on the sign-in screen. The reset email opens the app so you can choose a new password of at least 8 characters.

How do I join a job site?

Someone already on that job site shares an invite code, or creates a code for your email from Settings. Enter the code in the app. A code shared for everyone joins you as a superintendent. An email invite can name a position and expires in 14 days. The app does not send that email itself. The person who created the code has to give it to you.

Why do I not see a job site?

The board only shows job sites you have joined. Ask a teammate for the current invite code. Signing out and back in does not remove you from a job site.

What notifications will I get?

A standup reminder at the job site’s time, on the weekdays that job site chose, in that job site’s timezone. If the job site has Done alerts on, teammates can be told when a card moves to Done. You can mute other people’s Done alerts without muting your own celebration. The app asks the phone for notification permission. It does not send marketing messages.

Can I delete a job site or my account?

From Settings, someone who manages the job site can delete that site. Deleting it removes its cards, sprints, standups, and queued notifications. You can also leave a job site. Neither action deletes your account.

There is no button that deletes the whole account. Email boone-white67-jj3gjy@mail.grokbot.com from the address on the account and ask for the account to be deleted. Signing out removes this device’s push token when the phone can reach the server. The rest of the account stays until you ask for it to be deleted. Details are on the privacy policy.

Are there ads or tracking?

No. Team Scrum does not show ads, does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and does not sell data.

The app says to check email after I create an account.

A new account may need the confirmation email before the password works. Open that email on the phone and come back to the app, then sign in with the same email and password.
